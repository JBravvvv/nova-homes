Temperament
Every vet and pet professional who has met her in the past two years has called her the calmest husky they've seen.
Bring
Vet records and vaccination history, a vet or groomer reference letter, a PetScreening profile, and renter's insurance with pet liability.
Offer
A meet-and-greet with the owner, an added refundable pet deposit (Arizona caps total refundable deposits at 1.5 months' rent), or a pet addendum.
Ask
Husky bans usually come from the owner's insurance carrier. Ask whether the owner would accept your renter's policy naming them, or whether the ban is the manager's own rule.

Section 5 · Reference

How this list is built

  • Houses, townhomes, duplexes and build-to-rent home communities only. No apartment complexes.
  • Each home is checked against Nova: weight limits, "small dogs only" rules, and whether the manager's restricted-breed list names huskies. "Nova confirmed OK" means the breed list was read and huskies aren't on it, or there is no breed list.
  • Individual house listings go fast. Each home shows the date it was checked; open the listing before calling.
  • Move-in cost uses the lowest listed rent and only published fees. Arizona caps refundable deposits at 1.5 months' rent.
